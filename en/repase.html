Battery refurbishment

Cell replacement and component check based on the condition of your battery.

What refurbishment includes

First we assess whether the battery can be refurbished. That depends on the condition of the case, the cells and the BMS protection electronics, and on the availability of compatible parts.

  • Assessment of the original pack and its wiring
  • Selection of suitable cells and configuration
  • Replacement of damaged or worn parts as agreed
  • Final test and usage recommendations

The original case and usable parts can usually be kept. We always agree the scope of work and the price with you in advance.

Frequently asked questions

Can every battery be refurbished?

Not always. It depends on the battery's design, its condition and the compatibility of the parts.

Is the listed price final?

The price list is indicative. We'll prepare a specific quote after assessing the battery.

Should I just send the battery?

Please agree the hand-over with us first. If the battery is swollen or overheating, call us – it needs to be handled safely on an individual basis.

Before you contact us

What to send us

  • A photo of the battery label
  • Device type
  • Voltage and capacity
  • Description of the fault
Arrange an assessment Please don't take the battery apart for the photo.

Need advice?

Tell us what you need, or send a photo of the battery label and a description of the fault.
