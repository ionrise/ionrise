Testing and diagnostics

We find out the real condition of your battery before you decide on a repair.

Capacity, resistance and function

We measure the available capacity and other parameters depending on the battery type. We also check the wiring and the protection electronics.

  • Capacity measurement
  • Internal resistance and cell condition check
  • Assessment of the BMS and related faults

We agree the price and scope of the diagnostics in advance, including the terms of any follow-up refurbishment.
